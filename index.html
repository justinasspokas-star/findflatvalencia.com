Why expats struggle

Valencia is beautiful. The housing search can be frustrating.

Listings disappear fast, agencies do not always reply, Spanish paperwork is confusing and it is hard to judge an area from abroad. That is where we help.

  • Hours lost browsing listings and chasing agencies.
  • Unclear documents, deposits, contract terms and landlord requirements.
  • Risk of choosing a flat that looks good online but feels wrong in person.
  • Stress when you need housing before starting work, school or family life.

About us

We know the move
because we lived it.

We are an international family who moved to Valencia 10 years ago. Before buying our own property in the city, we rented, searched, compared neighbourhoods and went through the same questions many expats face when arriving in Spain.

That experience is why Find Flat Valencia was born: to help other expats move faster, easier and with more confidence. We know how stressful it can be to search from abroad, understand documents, speak with agencies, choose the right area and avoid wasting time on properties that do not fit.

Our goal is simple: honest local support, clear guidance and a smoother property search for people who want to start their Valencia life the right way.

FAQ

Questions expats ask before moving to Valencia.

Can you help if I am not in Spain yet?

Yes. We can review areas, contact agencies, coordinate viewings and send video feedback so you can make better decisions remotely.

Do you work for landlords or agencies?

No. Our role is to support you. We aim to give honest, independent advice and help you avoid bad-fit properties.

Can you guarantee I will get a flat?

No one can honestly guarantee that in a competitive market. What we can do is improve your search strategy, speed, documents and decision-making.

Do you help with buying property too?

Yes. We can help buyers understand areas, prices, property condition, negotiation and connect with trusted legal and finance professionals.
